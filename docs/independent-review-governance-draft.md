# Restricted independent review policy draft

For protocol rehearsal and governance review. The study lead and an independent custodian must approve a dated version and the applicable retention schedule before inviting participants. Register the exact policy bytes, instructions, plan and private-assignment commitment before opening intake.

## Roles, consent and conflicts

The custodian verifies qualification against the frozen criteria, checks authorship, collaboration, financial or supervisory conflicts, obtains consent, and retains only the evidence required by the agreed study. The reviewer receives one blinded packet over an individually controlled channel, states whether assignment information or other reviewers' answers were visible, and attests independence from the candidate and project. Reviewers can abstain or decline without supplying a relevance label. The analyst receives the frozen pseudonymous labels after collection closes. An independent auditor can reconcile private custody records and public commitments.

## Access and publication

Keep the reviewer-to-pseudonym mapping, random salt, assignment manifest, consent, conflicts, attestations and optional prose under access control. Use separate storage for contact details and labels. Never place identifying material in a Git repository, public packet, CLI argument or shared build log. Bind the hash of the instructions and each delivered packet to the registration. Return only an opaque receipt to the submitter. Publish only the approved summary commitments, revision, method and evidence class. Restrict pre-freeze reads to custody operations and withhold label totals and intermediate rankings.

## Retention, withdrawal and corrections

The consent form states the custodian, contact route, withdrawal deadline, retention dates for identities, prose, labels and backups, and the limits of withdrawal after irreversible anonymisation. Retain contact material only while needed to administer the study. Record receipt of a withdrawal privately, exclude it from analysis, delete prose and identifying material under the agreed schedule and arrange backup expiration. Preserve a non-identifying exclusion and commitment where consent permits. Corrections create a new labelled revision linked to its predecessor, recording the reason and scope. Preserve the original release and the fact that TEST was exposed. A correction cannot restore held-out status. Disclose any change to a published result.

## Incident response

On misdelivery, possible deanonymisation, conflicts, duplicate submissions or disclosed labels, pause affected assignments, restrict access, preserve a minimum private incident record and notify the custodian. Record affected packet hashes and exposure in the ledger, quarantine affected assignments, and apply the frozen exclusion rules before analysis. Document the scope and disposition in an appropriately redacted audit record. Resume only after the custodian and study lead record the disposition. A future study must bind prior exposures and must not reuse exposed candidates as fresh TEST cases.
