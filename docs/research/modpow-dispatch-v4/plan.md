## Frozen comparison

distinct product sites and one additional residue (3, 5 or 8 outputs).
Within each size run forward, reversed factors and outputs, no reuse, then
missing positive-modulus premise. Distractor bases differ. This exercises an
inventory in which a repeated binding can discriminate. Additionally rerun
the v3 homogeneous-base inputs as an explicitly retrospective stress control.
Do not tune the mechanism, weights or order after observing the new outcomes.
