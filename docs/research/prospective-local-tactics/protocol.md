# Prospective test of local reuse inside an unseen expression family

The previous [retained-library study](../prospective-retained-tactics/README.md) saw zero admissions on its two held-out families. We test whether a general matcher can cheaply identify a useful previously proved local rewrite inside a different outer expression. The previous C/D data are now development material. The new synthetic family deliberately includes ordinary primitive simplifications and opportunities for known local algebra; its population is not a sample of deployed workloads.

## Freeze and decision

The first complete public commit fixing solver, training inputs/receipts, case generator, independent oracle and evaluation procedure supplies the sole 40-character lowercase SHA seed. No E instances are generated before that freeze. Preserve its seed for reruns and disclose any fixture corrections. Product developers receive no E instances or outcomes while adapting their solver. The generator author knows the previous families and their mathematical results.

Evaluate 256 cases in order with 32 primitive steps, search depth 32, 200,000 theory-work units, 512 states, empty assumptions, a native upper node-count goal and the case's individual work cap. Start each solver with an independent cumulative deadline of **524,288 logical units**. Count its full applicable acquisition, loading, validation and preparation once, plus selection, search, in-search verification, separate witness replay and polynomial audit. Both solved and unsolved attempts advance the clock. Individual caps use selection, search and replay. Each arm runs all 256 cases. Repeat in two fresh JVMs with opposite arm order and compare the mathematical ledgers byte for byte.

Five arms are registered. SHARED is the optimized primitive solver from the previous study. PRIOR is its previously frozen whole-expression admission router with retained rules. LOCAL is the new local selector using the complete retained library. DROP uses that same selector and library/setup with all learned proposals disabled. LOCAL_OLD uses the same local mechanism with only the historical seven proved schemata and their applicable costs. Publish all five cold completion counts, per-case successes, full-stream work, setup, learned applications and paired outcomes. Keep any additional purely technical reference separate from this decision.

For the primary claim LOCAL must solve at least 10% more tasks by the deadline than the strongest of the four controls (positive comparator, using integer arithmetic). Also require a LOCAL solution before both deadlines where SHARED, PRIOR, LOCAL_OLD and DROP all fail at the same case budget, a learned rule occurs in the solution, and disabling that one rule while keeping all other proposals eligible prevents LOCAL from solving the same case under that cap. Retain every attempted single-rule knockout and failed search. A leading control of 233 or higher makes 10% improvement arithmetically impossible with 256 cases. Report it and retain both fixed budgets.

## Family E: squared residual of a weighted prediction

For each of 16 blocks and each triple (m, v, cap) from {2,3} × {0,1} × {256,768,2048,8192}, sort by hexadecimal SHA-256 of UTF-8 `seed/order/E/block/m/v/cap`, ties by m, v and cap numerically. In block b at zero-based sorted position s use i=16*b+s and n=110001+i. Order j=0..m−1 by SHA-256 of `seed/vars/E/i/j`, ties by j. Numeric hash inputs use ordinary decimals.

At position j take a loading from a,b,c; a reading from e,f,g; an increment from j,k,l; a gain from p,q,r; and an adjustment from s,t,u. The ordinary component is reading*gain + increment*gain. If v=1 the first permuted component is (gain+adjustment)*(gain−adjustment) + adjustment*adjustment. Multiply each component by its loading and sum the weighted terms left-associatively in permuted order. The expression is ((h+n) minus this prediction) squared. The target is at most **8*m+5−2*v** nodes. Primitive factoring reduces an ordinary component to (reading+increment)*gain; the alternate reduces to gain squared. These supply independent exact polynomial and node-count oracles. Only the source, node goal and standard budgets enter the solver. The complete squared residual differs from earlier sums of squared radii, repeated interpolation, cascaded products, determinants, Horner/dot products and quadrature.

Independently regenerate each expression, its exact polynomial and its size target. Compare coefficient multisets, which are invariant under variable renaming, against previous panels, training/calibration sources and other E cases; abort on a collision. Check the original and final polynomials, every reported named rule step, per-case node count, replay, paid clocks and ablations. Internal search and fit charges are recorded by the Java implementation. Local algebra can overlap with earlier training by design.
