# Prospective test of learned tactic selection under a fixed budget

This protocol builds on [PR #1431](https://github.com/carstenartur/Regelsuche/pull/1431), revision `34d873df4964e2584a2a5c8df063b30cc45927e4`. We test whether a general Java solver can acquire tactics from its own earlier proofs, learn where to try them from measured useful and unhelpful attempts, and finish materially more verified tasks. The public Horner/dot-product and quadrature results remain development material. They cannot establish transfer in this study.

## Decisions before opening the held-out inputs

1. The unit of transfer is a whole expression family. Register both families below before running them. A separate developer chooses the training, promotion and policy-calibration examples using public development material without access to the new family formulas. Record the exact sources, split, inventory, model, calibrated policy, charges and witnesses. Exclude complete final expressions and family templates from training and calibration. Report repeated local mathematical relationships. Abort if the declared separation fails. Final instances are related within each family.
2. Commit the evaluator, trained-solver recipe, complete training and calibration material, product code and independent checker. The sole final seed is the full lowercase SHA of the first publicly committed complete freeze. The solver sees only the input AST, node-count goal, empty assumptions and ordinary resource limits. No family labels, stratum IDs, final outcomes or generator metadata enter selection or proof formation.
3. Run all registered tasks and arms in two fresh JVMs with opposite arm order. Retain gains and losses, full paths, accounting for failed attempts, and timing observations separately. No new settings, seed, exclusions or solver tuning after opening FINAL. Calibration and training remain frozen between families.
4. For **each family separately**, the primary result is the number of verified tasks finished by a cold-start cumulative deadline of **1,048,576 logical work units**. Every arm pays its own full training, failed learning attempts, promotion, validation, policy calibration, model loading/preparation and actual selection, search, in-search verification, separate terminal replay and polynomial audit. Charge once per arm per family, including previous acquisition for historical learned arms. Ablations retain the setup and selection work of the corresponding learned configuration. Both successful and unsuccessful queries advance the clock. Run all 512 even after the deadline.
5. Preregister eight arms: primitive BASE, the frozen #1431 snapshot with automatic routing AUTO_OLD, #1431 FAST_OLD forced on, the existing automatic router AUTO_FRESH and FAST_FRESH on the freshly acquired library, SHARED_FRESH applying the new proof checker to the same fresh library without the new learned policy, POLICY combining the new checker and policy, and NO_TACTICS retaining that configuration while disabling its acquired tactic proposals. Record all actual setups and publish how fallback and removal operate. Use the same primitive inventory and goal and allow ordinary search and complete independent proof checks in every arm.
6. Success requires at least **10% more solutions in each of the two families** than the best of the seven prespecified controls in that family: `10 * POLICY >= 11 * max(controls)`, with a positive comparator and a strict improvement. No pooling across families and no post-hoc budget. Also require at least one previously unsolved input per family where POLICY finishes by both deadlines with a checked learned step, BASE, AUTO_OLD, FAST_OLD and NO_TACTICS fail under the same **individual** budget, and removing one specifically identified, used acquired schema alone makes the same policy fail at that individual budget. Other schemata remain eligible. Keep all removal attempts and their costs as diagnostic experiments, separate from the primary race. Report new coverage, lost coverage and matched paths, any cases that require removing several schemata together, and single-ablation successes or failures. A gain earned only from processing earlier cases faster cannot serve as this witness.

## Frozen sources

Each family independently contains 32 balanced blocks of 16 tasks. Strata are all pairs of depth/term count m in {2,3} and orientation o in {0,1}, crossed with individual work cap c in {256,768,2048,8192}. In block b order by ascending hexadecimal SHA-256 of UTF-8 `seed/order/F/b/m/o/c` with F equal to A or B, ordinary decimal integers, and tie break m then o then c. Let i=16*b+slot. Permute j=0..m-1 by SHA-256 of `seed/vars/F/i/j`, breaking ties by j. Keep #1431's fixed query limits outside total work: 32 states, depth 32, the existing 200,000 and 512 limits in the same order as the historical budget constructor. All queries use the native node-count goal.

**A. Tensor-product interpolation of constant data.** Set w=a+(50001+i) and I0=w. For level k use the k-th permuted index j. Take coordinate u from p,q,r at j when o=0; when o=1 take the sum of that coordinate and the corresponding coordinate from s,t,u. Form I(k+1) by duplicating the previous expression, `I(k)*(1-u)+I(k)*u` with parentheses enforcing that tree. The goal for I(m) is at most 3 nodes. The combination is the ordinary repeated linear interpolation of identical values. Both parameterizations admit a primitive solution by factoring, cancellation and multiplication by one.

**B. Sum of squared radii reconstructed from semicircles.** Associate each permuted index j with a radius from b,c,d and an offset from e,f,g at j. Replace the first permuted radius by its sum with 60001+i, prior to changing the order of terms. For each pair (r,d) take `(r+d)*(r-d)+d*d`. Sum these terms left-associatively in the permuted order for o=0 and reverse that order for o=1. Each squared height and squared offset sum to the squared radius. The bound for the whole sum is 4*m+1 nodes. The formula derives from the geometry. The canonical output is not supplied to the solver.

Both tests are synthetic exact polynomial problems. Validate that every final input is distinct under variable renaming from training, validation and calibration, from the published panels and from other final cases by a sufficient exact coefficient check or an equally strong independent exclusion. If earlier panels have overlapping signatures, require disjointness only across the new boundary. Report both total budget coverage and locally unsolved cases. Verify the final and intermediate proof polynomials, achieved structural quality, auditable scores and charges, and reconstruct arm decisions independently from the row records. Report any limitation on independently reconstructing internal implementation counters. Compare logical work to logical work. Timing claims require separate evidence.

## Development comparison

On the public 1,024-case Horner/dot-product stream, measure search work and terminal replay separately and isolate changes in policy and proof checking where possible. Include the policy training bill; hold the library and proof quality constant across matched configurations and disclose the setup for each. The old FAST reference is 708 completions and 752,130 full units. A reduction of 8,648 units in the previous excess terminal replay, with other work held constant, would double the previously measured 8,648-unit advantage over the same optimized engine without learned proposals. This is a diagnostic scale, not a second way to pass the primary test.
