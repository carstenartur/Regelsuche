# Retaining learned tactics and deciding when a proposal is worth its cost

The parent study is [PR #1432](https://github.com/carstenartur/Regelsuche/pull/1432), study freeze `c7141702e091ff94b5ed2e3c5af5b5d2d66136dd`. Its two published families A and B and the older mixed Horner/dot-product stream are development data. We ask whether retaining checked rules and a cheap, evidence-based admission decision buys additional verified solutions in two unseen expression families. The new family author inspected the public inventories to include plausible opportunities and misses. No FINAL results or generated instances were available when defining the generators. This is a targeted transfer test of a general solver on synthetic mathematical expressions.

## Freeze and accounting

1. Publish this protocol first. Develop the solver and calibrate only on archived public development data; record the exact inputs and paid costs of any new acquisition or fitting. Publish the complete implementation, fixed libraries, calibration recipe/material, generator, comparison and independent checker. The sole FINAL seed is the full lowercase Git SHA-1 of the first public complete freeze. No fitting, threshold changes, deletions or alternative seed after generating FINAL. Record any repair needed to run the fixed experiment. The solver receives the ordinary expression, empty assumptions, maximum output node count and standard budget. Generator labels, case indices and expected expressions are withheld.
2. Separately score each family through one cold cumulative deadline of **524,288** logical work units. Include acquisition and validation of every library used, retained-library preparation, loading, calibration and selection, search work including in-search verification, independent terminal replay and exact polynomial audits. Record sums and costs even for failed tasks. For the secondary post-setup diagnostic start the clock at zero, retain every per-row selection, search, replay and audit charge, and count verified rows finished by 524,288 units. Run all 512 queries irrespective of either deadline. A verified case must meet its structural goal and individual query cap (selection + search + final replay); the independent polynomial audit advances the family clock. Publish elapsed observations separately, without granting any credit for unmeasured execution time.
3. Freeze ten arms: primitive search (BASE), former automatic policy on the historical library (AUTO_OLD), former fixed selection on the historical library with the improved checker (CHECKED_OLD), the previous fully fitted policy on the fresh library (PREVIOUS_POLICY), new admission with only the fresh or only the historical library (FRESH_ONLY, OLD_ONLY), combined historical and fresh libraries with the new admission and no further acquisition (PRIOR_UNION), the retained candidate library always enabled (RETAINED_FORCED), that library with new admission (RETAINED), and the same prepared admission with all learned proposals suppressed (DROP_ALL). RETAINED, RETAINED_FORCED and DROP_ALL additionally attempt general acquisition from archived primitive development tasks. They pay all attempts, including rejected acquisitions, use the augmented library when promotion succeeds and otherwise retain the earlier union. Pay both parent acquisitions and composition work for arms using the two libraries. Single-library admissions pay their own fitting. Match RETAINED and DROP_ALL on setup, learned selection and available primitive rules. Report the counts of supported distinct schema IDs coming from each library and the overlap. Identical schemata do not count twice.
4. For a strong result require, **in each family separately**, at least 10% more verified completions at the cold deadline than the best of all nine other arms. Test `10 * RETAINED >= 11 * max(other arms)` with a positive best control and strict improvement. Also require at least one timely individually solved case per family where BASE, AUTO_OLD, CHECKED_OLD, PREVIOUS_POLICY and DROP_ALL all fail under the original case budget and removing one specifically identified used tactic alone from RETAINED makes it fail. Other tactics remain eligible with full learned setup and selection still charged. Run removal on all eligible cases/used IDs, report every outcome, and distinguish historic, recent and shared schemata. Removing all rules is insufficient by itself. A best control of 466 or more among 512 would place 10% improvement outside the available ceiling. Report that outcome if observed, retaining the fixed deadline. Publish paired wins/losses, failures, work and margin even if both tests fail. Report separate comparisons to the previously deployed/strongest historical controls; full or single-library controls can tie the winning candidate even when it improves on its predecessor.
5. Run FINAL twice in fresh JVMs with opposite arm order. Independently reconstruct case sources, check full mathematical separation from all earlier panels and training/calibration inputs (a sufficient invariant under variable renaming is acceptable), verify polynomial values and each published rule-labelled witness step, node counts, per-case budgets and cumulative deadlines. Prospective conclusions require identical deterministic mathematical results in both runs. Related tasks in the same family are controlled variants. No inference to a frequency distribution of real programs is implied.

## Two unseen expression families

Each family has 32 blocks of the 16 triples `(m,o,cap)` with m=2 or 3, o=0 or 1 and cap in {256,768,2048,8192}. Within each block b sort by ascending hexadecimal SHA-256 of the UTF-8 text `seed/order/F/b/m/o/cap`, breaking ties numerically by m, o and cap. Use ordinary decimal integers and F=C or D. The resulting zero-based index is i=16*b+slot. Sort 0..k−1 by SHA-256 of `seed/vars/F/i/j`, tie break by j, taking k=m for C and k=4 for D. All source parentheses follow the constructions below. Constrain all searches to 32 primitive steps, depth 32, 200,000 units of theory work, 512 states and the given individual cap.

**C. Cascaded split-and-recombine stages.** The output of serial multiplicative stages is the product of their gains. Each stage divides a total into a remainder and one or two subflows, all with the same gain. For j=0..m−1 choose gain g from a,b,c, total t from e,f,g, portion p from j,k,l and second portion q from n,o,p, respectively by index. In this notation italic g, t, p and q are metavariables. Replace the gain at the first permuted stage by `(g+(70001+i))`. For o=0 write each stage as `g*(t-p)+g*p`. For o=1 write `(g*((t-p)-q)+g*q)+g*p`. Form the left-associated product of parenthesized stages in permuted order. The query goal is at most 4*m+1 nodes (the product of m gain–total pairs with one additive gain). This tests local distributive identities across a previously unseen multiplicative stage topology.

**D. Oriented area from archived planar component measurements.** The signed area of two planar vectors is the determinant `X0*X1-X2*X3`, with coordinate slots in the corresponding determinant order. Use unexpanded coordinates p,q,r,s, slot gains a,b,c,d, readings e,f,g,h and adjustments j,k,l,m by slot j. Expand the first m slots of the four-element permutation and leave the other slots as their unexpanded coordinates. Add 90001+i to the gain of the first expanded slot and parenthesize that sum. Ordinarily a touched reading is `t*g+u*g` (two contributions sharing a calibration gain). With o=1 only the first expanded slot has the alternate reading `g*(t-u)+g*u` (a total reconstructed from a remainder and correction). Take `(X0*X1)-(X2*X3)` with each expanded coordinate parenthesized. The target is at most 9+4*m−2*o nodes: the determinant on four leaves takes seven nodes, the gain adjustment two more, each ordinary expansion collected into `(t+u)*g` adds four, and collecting the alternate instead adds two. The o=0 stratum has only ordinary shared-calibration products. Retain and count every miss.

Both families deliberately reuse elementary local identities. Distinct whole-case polynomials, distinct final versus training signatures, byte-identical arm ledgers and checked witnesses are separate requirements. The shift at one gain makes each generated polynomial identifiable; the independent checker must still test every instance.
