# Knowledge Packs

Regelsuche knowledge packs live in `regelsuche-core/src/main/resources/rules/packs/*.rules.yaml`.
They record the rule pattern, provenance, license, review status, risk level, and validation examples.

Expanded SymPy packs are available for polynomial, trigonometry, rational, and logarithm identities. Only `VALIDATED` and `REVIEWED` rules are registrable; candidates stay out of enabled packs until reviewed.
